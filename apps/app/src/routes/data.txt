# Where your drawings are saved

## Without an account

Drawings are saved in this browser on this device. They aren’t uploaded unless you share them.

Clearing your browser’s data can erase these drawings. Browsers can also remove saved data automatically, so use an account for drawings you want to keep.

## If you use Safari

Safari can erase drawings saved only in your browser after seven days of Safari use without interacting with Splatterboard. Sign in to keep drawings you want to save in your account.

Read about Safari’s storage policy: https://webkit.org/blog/10218/full-third-party-cookie-blocking-and-more/#7-day-cap-on-all-script-writeable-storage

## With an account

When you sign in or create an account, drawings on this device are added to your account. Changes save automatically when you’re online. Sign in on another device to find your drawings there.

## Sharing

Sharing uploads your drawing and creates a link. Anyone with that link can open it. Saving a drawing to your account doesn’t share it.

## Signing out

Splatterboard finishes saving your changes before removing your account’s drawings from this browser. Your drawings stay in your account.

If saving fails, your drawings remain on the device so you can try again.

## Your password

Accounts use a username and password, with no email address. There’s no password reset, so use a password manager.
